The Path to the Podium

Competition Pathway

We guide athletes systematically through the hierarchy of competitive sport fencing.

Stage 1

Local Club Events

Initial tournament structure, pool sheet navigation, referee rules validation, and local club matches.

Stage 2

Regional Championships

RYCs, SYCs, and ROCs. Earn regional placement points and qualify for the summer national tournaments.

Stage 3

National Championships

USA Fencing North American Cups (NACs) and Summer Nationals. Competitive recruitment parameters.

Stage 4

International Competition

FIE Cadet & Junior World Cups, Grand Prix events, Senior World Championships, and Olympic qualification events.
